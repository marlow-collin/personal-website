Master's Thesis02/2025 – 08/2025
Specialising an Open Source LLM for Marine Engineering Applications within Hardware Constraints using LoRA Fine-Tuning
  • Fine-tuned Microsoft Phi-3 on a domain dataset (10,000 entries, marine engineering & oceanography) via LoRA and supervised fine-tuning.
  • Trained on an RTX 3090 Ti (24 GB); inference targeted under 8 GB to simulate consumer hardware.
  • Improved METEOR from 0.300 to 0.348 on the domain benchmark with no degradation on the general MMLU benchmark - domain knowledge gained without catastrophic forgetting.
  • Second strand: adapted the model to xVal number encoding; found LoRA fine-tuning insufficient and full pre-training necessary.

Simulation and Prediction of Saliency Maps for People with Impaired Visual Perception using Convolutional Neural Networks
  • Adapted DeepGazeIIE to predict attention patterns under simulated impairments (reduced contrast, colour-vision deficiency, macular degeneration), using only the original image plus a modification flag.
  • Trained on 10,000 images, evaluated on 4,012, scored with the SIM metric across single and combined modifications.
  • Established that the approach does not meaningfully learn impairment-specific saliency, and analysed why - a deliberately reported negative result.
